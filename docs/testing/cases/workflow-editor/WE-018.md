# Mini map and controls panel toggle from Options

## Scenario

The Options items **Display a mini map** and **Display the controls panel** show and hide the mini map widget and the zoom controls.

## Steps

1. Sign in as an administrator and open `wp-admin/admin.php?page=future_workflow_editor`.
2. Dismiss the welcome guide with **Get started** if shown.
3. Open the Options menu. For **Display a mini map** and **Display the controls panel**, turn each off if on. Confirm no mini map and no zoom controls are on the canvas.
4. Click **Display a mini map** to turn it on.
5. Look at the canvas.
6. Click **Display a mini map** again to turn it off.
7. Look at the canvas.
8. Click **Display the controls panel** to turn it on.
9. Look at the canvas for zoom in, zoom out and fit view controls.
10. Click **Display the controls panel** again to turn it off.
11. Look at the canvas.

## Expected result

1. With **Display a mini map** on, the mini map widget is visible; off, it is hidden.
2. With **Display the controls panel** on, the React Flow controls (zoom in, zoom out, fit view) are visible; off, they are hidden.
3. Toggling one does not change the other.

## Edge cases

- Either item may be on by default; step 3 normalizes this.
- Both on at the same time is allowed.
