# Advanced Settings and Developer mode reveal extra panels

## Scenario

**Advanced Settings** reveals inspector controls hidden by default. **Developer mode** reveals a developer information panel on the selected node. Turning each off hides them again.

## Steps

1. Sign in as an administrator and open `wp-admin/post-new.php?post_type=ppfuture_workflow`.
2. Dismiss the welcome guide with **Get started** if shown.
3. Pick **Post is published**, then add **Move post to status** from the trigger output.
4. Open the Options menu. If **Advanced Settings** or **Developer mode** is on, turn it off.
5. Select the **Move post to status** node and note the inspector fields and that no developer information panel is shown.
6. Open the Options menu and click **Advanced Settings** to turn it on.
7. Look at the inspector for the selected node.
8. Open the Options menu and click **Advanced Settings** to turn it off.
9. Look at the inspector.
10. Open the Options menu and click **Developer mode** to turn it on.
11. Look at the selected node.
12. Open the Options menu and click **Developer mode** to turn it off.
13. Look at the selected node.

## Expected result

1. With **Advanced Settings** on, at least one settings control that was hidden is now visible in the inspector.
2. With it off, that control is hidden again.
3. With **Developer mode** on, a developer information panel for the selected node is visible.
4. With it off, that panel is hidden again.

## Edge cases

- Specific field names are not asserted; compare the inspector before and after.
- Toggling one option does not change the other.
