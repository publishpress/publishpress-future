# Debug Logs in PublishPress Future

[PublishPress Future](https://publishpress.com/links/future) has a “Debug Logs” feature to record details of what is happening with Actions and Workflows.

To enable the logs, go to “Action Settings”, then “Diagnostics and Tools”. Click the “Enable Debugging” button,

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-future/enable-debugging-1-1a113fd14c.webp)

There will be a new “Debug” tab that contains your logs. The next time an actions or workflow runs, the details will be recorded here.

![Debug logs](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-future/debug-reports-9d373e3959.webp)
