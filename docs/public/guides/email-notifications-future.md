# Email Notifications for Actions

The [PublishPress Future](https://publishpress.com/links/future) plugin can send you email notifications when automatic actions are taken on your posts. The content of the email will look like the image below.

![Post Expirator Email](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-future/post-expirator-email-89881bf5ab.webp)

*Post Expirator Email*

You can control who received the emails by going to “Future Actions” in the WordPress admin menu and choosing the “Notifications” tab. You can also customize who receives the emails by [setting the defaults for each post type](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/defaults-for-post-types/).

![Future Action Notifications](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-future/future-notifications-477d2d80dd.webp)

*Emails Post Expirator*
